## Architecture

- **Basílica de la Sagrada Família**, designed by **Antoni Gaudí** (1852–1926) and built by the
  architects who continued his work. This is an independent fan recreation of the interior; it is
  not affiliated with or endorsed by the Basílica.
- **Measurements and the building's programme** (the 7.5 m module, the heights of the naves,
  crossing and apse, the choir galleries at 15 m, the four stones of the columns and their
  double-twist rule, the hyperboloid skylights, the stained-glass programme) were taken from the
  Basílica's official **Information Booklet 9, "Interior of the Basilica"**. The booklet itself is
  not included.

## Lilith (Terminal Dogma, off by default)

- **Evangelion**, Lilith and the Spear of Longinus: *Neon Genesis Evangelion*, created by
  Hideaki Anno. © khara. Fan work, non-commercial; not affiliated with or endorsed by khara.
- **3D model:** based on ["Lilith – Evangelion"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lilith-evangelion-c8c4c0f24f854f8084f8038a34057d9d)
  by [solodovnykov](https://sketchfab.com/solodovnykov), licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (`assets/lilith/LICENSE-model.txt`).
  **Changes made** (`tools/lilith/`): reduced from 2.6 million to about 377 thousand triangles;
  the arms and ribcage softened; the root-like lower body lengthened; the head and neck smoothed;
  the mask repainted lilac with the inverted Seele triangle; the cross repainted red with fine
  cracks; the nails rebuilt as hexagonal bolts; two LCL streams and a pool added.

## Music

- **Built-in recording:** Ludwig van Beethoven, Symphony No. 9 in D minor, Op. 125, fourth
  movement (words: Friedrich Schiller, "An die Freude"), excerpt from a 1923 acoustic 78 rpm
  recording. Public domain (Wikimedia Commons:
  [Ode to Joy 1923.opus](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ode_to_Joy_1923.opus),
  digitised by the YouTube channel "Old Record Music"). Details in `assets/music/SOURCE.txt`.
- Any other music is your own, read from the folder you choose; nothing from it is included here.

## Software

- [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT) draws the basilica.
- [music-metadata](https://github.com/Borewit/music-metadata) (MIT) reads the tags of your music.
- [ffmpeg-static](https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static) provides [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)
  (LGPL/GPL), used to play formats browsers can't and to make cover pictures. It is downloaded by
  `npm install`, not included here.
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org) (Cycles) computed the baked light in `bake/` (`tools/bake.py`)
  and the changes to the Lilith model.
- The album cover of the built-in recording is a render of this project.
